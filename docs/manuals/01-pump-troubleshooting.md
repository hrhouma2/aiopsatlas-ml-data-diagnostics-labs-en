# Pump troubleshooting guide (NorthPeak PX series)

Applies to machines of type `pump` (M001 to M010).

## Normal operating ranges

| Signal | Normal | Warning | Stop the pump |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casing temperature | 25 to 60 C | 60 to 75 C | above 75 C |
| Vibration | below 4.5 mm/s | 4.5 to 7.0 mm/s | above 7.0 mm/s |
| Outlet pressure | 5.5 to 7.5 bar | 4.5 to 5.5 bar | below 4.5 bar |
| Power at full load | 40 to 50 kW | 50 to 55 kW | above 55 kW |

## Symptom: vibration rising over several days

1. Check the coupling alignment with a dial gauge. Misalignment above 0.1 mm causes vibration.
2. Listen to the drive shaft bearing. A grinding noise means bearing wear.
3. Check the mounting bolts. Loose bolts add vibration of 1 to 2 mm/s.
4. If vibration stays above 7.0 mm/s, stop the pump and open a **high** severity incident.

Bearing wear is the most common fault on pumps. Replace bearings every 20,000 running hours.

## Symptom: pressure drop with normal load

1. Look for a puddle under the outlet flange. A wet flange means a seal leak.
2. Check the inlet strainer. A blocked strainer lowers pressure by 1 to 2 bar.
3. Compare the reading with a manual gauge. A frozen sensor reads the same value for days.

## Symptom: overheating

1. Check the coolant level. Top up if below the minimum mark.
2. Clean the cooling fins. Dust cuts cooling by up to 30 percent.
3. Lower the load to 50 percent and wait 20 minutes. If the temperature does not fall, stop the pump.

## Restart procedure after a stop

1. Wait until the casing is below 40 C.
2. Reset the thermal switch.
3. Run at 30 percent load for 10 minutes.
4. Log the restart in the incident record.
