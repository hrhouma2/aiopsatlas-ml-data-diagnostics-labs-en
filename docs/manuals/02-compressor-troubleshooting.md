# Compressor troubleshooting guide (NorthPeak CX series)

Applies to machines of type `compressor` (M011 to M020).

## Normal operating ranges

| Signal | Normal | Warning | Stop the compressor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casing temperature | 35 to 75 C | 75 to 90 C | above 90 C |
| Vibration | below 6.0 mm/s | 6.0 to 9.0 mm/s | above 9.0 mm/s |
| Tank pressure | 8.0 to 10.0 bar | 7.0 to 8.0 bar | below 7.0 bar |
| Power at full load | 70 to 85 kW | 85 to 95 kW | above 95 kW |

Compressors are the most powerful machines on site. They run hot by design. A temperature of 70 C is normal for a compressor and would be a warning for a pump.

## Symptom: breaker trips

1. Do not reset the breaker more than twice. A third trip means an electrical fault.
2. Check the motor insulation with a megohmmeter. Below 1 megohm, replace the motor.
3. Open the junction box. Look for a loose or burnt terminal.
4. Check fault code on the control panel. Codes E20 to E39 are electrical. Codes E40 to E59 are thermal.

## Symptom: pressure does not reach 8 bar

1. Listen for air leaks at the hose fittings. Use soapy water to find small leaks.
2. Check the intake filter. Replace it if it is grey or oily.
3. Check the unloader valve. A stuck valve keeps the compressor in idle mode.

## Symptom: high vibration

1. Check the belt tension. A loose belt slaps and causes vibration.
2. Check the motor mount bolts.
3. Inspect the crankshaft bearing. Bearing wear on a compressor gets worse quickly, within 5 to 7 days.

## Maintenance schedule

- Every 500 hours: change the oil, check the belt.
- Every 2,000 hours: replace the intake filter, check the valves.
- Every 8,000 hours: replace the bearings.
